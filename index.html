Mission Statement

At JG Services & projects, our mission is to deliver high-quality sand supply and minor construction services with professionalism and reliability, while maintaining full compliance with mining, safety, and environmental regulations. We are commited to building strong, long-term partnerships with brick manufacturers, local businesses, contractors, and residential clients, empowering women in business, and creating sustainable employment opportunities within our community. Through continuous growth and expansion, we aim to meet the increasing demand for construction materials in the region.

Vision Statement

Our vision is to become the leading women-owned sand mining and construction support company in Limpopo, recognized for excellence, integrity, sustainability, and meaningful contribution to economic growth in Mokopane and surrounding communities.
