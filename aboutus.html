Who We Are

JG Services is a trusted provider of sand mining, bulk material supply, and construction services. Since 2001, we have built a reputation for reliability, regulatory compliance, and customer satisfaction across residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects.

BBBEE & Women Empowerment Positioning

JG Services & Projects is a proudly South African, 100% women-led enterprise based in Mokopane, operating within the Limpopo region. As a company founded and directed by two women entrepreneurs, we are deeply commited to advancing inclusive economic participation and transformation within the mining and construction sectors.

We align our operations with the principles and objectives of Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (BBBEE), actively promoting:

Black ownership and leadership at executive and operational levels

Women empowerment in traditionally male-dominated levels

Local job creation and skills development

Enterprise and supplier development within our community

Sustainable economic contribution to regional growth

Through our sand mining operations and construction services, we strive to create opportunities that uplift local communities, support small businesses, and foster long-term economic resilience.

JG Services & Projects stands as a symbol of transformation, demonstrating that women-led businesses can successfully operate, compete, and lead within the mining and infrastructure value chain.

We are commited not only to delivering professional and reliable services, but also to building a legacy of empowerment, compliance, and inclusive growth.
